# Role-play prep with a judge panel

1. Write your opener yourself. No AI for this part.
2. Paste this into your AI tool, then paste your scenario and your opener:

   > Play a panel of three DECA judges: a small-business owner, a bank manager and a marketing director. I'll paste my role-play scenario and my opening minute. Each judge scores me 1 to 10, quotes my weakest sentence, and asks me one tough question. Don't rewrite it for me.

3. Fix the weakest sentence yourself. Answer the tough questions out loud.
4. Run it again. Repeat until you're bored.
5. The check: does your opener tell the client one thing they don't already know, and end with a question? If they could have said it themselves, cut it.

Competition day has no devices. The words have to be yours.
