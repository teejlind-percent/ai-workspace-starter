# Brief

1. Ask for the project name if you don't have it. Copy `projects/_template/` to `projects/YYYY-MM-short-name/`.
2. Fill in what you already know from `context/` and the conversation.
3. Interview the user two or three questions at a time for each empty field: WHAT, WHO IT'S FOR, WHAT WE KNOW, WHAT GOOD LOOKS LIKE, BRAND, RULES, DUE, DONE MEANS.
4. Push back on vague answers. "Students" isn't an audience; "freshmen who've never heard of DECA, on their phones between classes" is.
5. Never fill a field with a guess. Unknown stays [blank], listed at the bottom as an open question.
6. Show the finished brief and ask: "Could a new intern do great work from this?" Fix it until the answer is yes.
