# Question bank

Pick two or three per round. Go deeper on anything vague, surprising or emotional. You don't need all of them.

## 1. Who you are
- What do you do? School, grade, job, clubs, teams, whatever takes up your week.
- Walk me through a normal week. Where does the time actually go?
- What do people come to you for?

## 2. What you're working toward
- What do you want to be true by the end of this year? Make it specific enough that we'd both know if it happened.
- What about this month?
- What's the bigger thing all of this is for, even if it's fuzzy?
- What would make this a great year, not just a good one?

## 3. What's on your plate now
- What are you working on right now? Any deadlines?
- What's stuck, and why?
- What do you keep putting off?
- If I could take one thing off your plate this week, what would it be?

## 4. Who you make things for
- Who sees your work? A judge, a teacher, customers, a boss, followers?
- What does each one care about most? What makes them stop paying attention?
- Who has to approve things before they go out?

## 5. How you work
- When and where do you do your best work? On what device?
- Do you like a plan up front, or do you figure it out as you go?
- How do you know when something's done?
- What do you always underestimate?

## 6. How you want answers
- Short and direct, or full explanations? Bullets or paragraphs?
- When you ask me something, do you want the answer first or the reasoning first?
- How blunt should I be when your work isn't good yet?
- Should I ask questions before I start, or take a shot and let you correct me?

## 7. How you sound
- Paste two or three things you wrote that sound like you: an email, a speech, a post, an essay.
- Words or phrases you use a lot?
- Words or phrases that make you cringe? (Common answers: "delve," "leverage," "in today's fast-paced world," anything that sounds like a brochure.)
- Anything that makes AI writing obvious to you?

## 8. Who the AI should be
- If I were a person on your team, who would I be? A coach, an editor, an assistant, a tough mentor?
- How hard should I push back when I think you're wrong?
- What's annoyed you most about AI answers so far?
- Any sense of humor you want, or none?

## 9. People
- Who do you work with most? First names and roles only.
- Who do you report to, or who grades or judges your work?
- Anyone I should handle with extra care?

## 10. Rules you live under
- Does your school, job or competition have rules about using AI? What are they, exactly?
- Is there anything you're not allowed to share, or don't want stored here?
- Anything that should never leave this folder?

## 11. Where you're getting better
- What skill are you trying to build this year?
- Where should I coach you (ask questions, critique, point out the weak spot) instead of doing it for you?
- Where is it fine for me to just do the work?
- How will you know you've gotten better?

## 12. What good looks like
- Show me something you made that you're proud of. What makes it good?
- Show me something that fell flat. What went wrong?
- Whose work do you admire? What do they do that you don't yet?

## Closing questions (always ask)
- What didn't I ask that I should know?
- What's one thing you want me to always do? One thing I should never do?
