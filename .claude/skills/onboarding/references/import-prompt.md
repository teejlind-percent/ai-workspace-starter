# Import prompt

Give this to the user verbatim. They paste it into the AI they've been using, then paste the answer back here.

---

I'm setting up a new AI workspace and want to bring over what you know about how I work. Please write a summary of what you've learned about me from our conversations, but ONLY about my work, school and goals. Do not include anything personal: no health information, no family or relationship details, no money, no home address or contact info, nothing about my life outside work and school.

Capture:

1. My role: school, job, clubs or teams, what I work on
2. My goals and the projects I've asked you about
3. Tools and apps I use
4. How I like answers: length, format, tone, how blunt
5. How I write and talk: words I use, things I've asked you to stop doing
6. Tasks I ask for help with over and over
7. People I've mentioned, by first name and role only
8. Mistakes you've made that I corrected, and what I wanted instead

Format it as a clean list I can paste into another AI. Keep it factual. If you're not sure whether something is personal, leave it out. If you're guessing, say so.

---

When it comes back: treat every line as a draft. Confirm it with the user during the interview, and drop anything they don't want in this folder.
